Check one experiment against the standards of good experimental practice, using only the experiment inventory entry and the original text supplied. This is a focused check of one experiment, not a review of the whole study. Do not use outside knowledge of this manuscript.

Input: the inventory entry for the experiment, the original text of the pages the entry cites, and the methods context. Blocks start with source IDs.

Go through the items below. Report an item only when it applies to this experiment. For each reported item give status ok, concern or not_assessable (not_assessable when the supplied text does not allow a judgement; say what is missing). A concern needs a verbatim quote of at most 200 characters from a cited block that shows the practice in question, or, for something not stated, a quote of the sentence where it would be expected (for example the legend or the methods sentence for this experiment). Missing information is a concern about reporting, not proof that the work was not done; phrase it as not stated in the supplied text.

Items:
- unit_of_inference: which unit the test treats as N (cells, fields, genes, reads, wells, plates, donors, animals, independent experiments), whether that matches the claim, and whether many observations from few samples inflate significance.
- replicate_independence: whether the stated replicates are independent biological replicates, technical repeats or repeated measures of one sample; pairing, nesting and shared controls.
- scale_and_model: whether the scale and model fit the data. Ratios and fold changes belong on a log scale; count-derived proportions need their numerators and denominators; skewed quantities (distances, intensities, read counts) need a transformation or a rank-based method; assumptions of the test; a plotting axis is not an analysis.
- reference_normalization: values normalized to a control or reference set to 1 or 100 percent and then tested against that reference; the reference then has no variance and the test is invalid or inflated.
- uncertainty_display: whether error bars are defined, whether they show inferential uncertainty (confidence intervals, or SEM explicitly labelled) or only dispersion (SD), whether individual points are shown, and whether effect sizes with intervals are given.
- time_course_design: independent or repeated samples across time points, whether the hypothesis is a trajectory or a defined contrast, and pointwise tests without a stated contrast or multiplicity control.
- multiplicity_and_thresholds: many outcomes, genes, contrasts or time points tested without error control; thresholds that are hand set or fixed after seeing the data; choice of post hoc procedure.
- representativity: for images, blots, gels and tracks, whether the shown example is representative of stated independent repeats and whether a quantification exists.
- controls_completeness: controls that the design needs for its alternative explanations and that the text does not mention (input, mock, vehicle, loading, specificity, rescue, expression or knockdown verification, matched total input), and whether a control addresses what it is cited for.
- batch_and_confounding: treatment confounded with batch, run, plate, date, cell line, sex or donor.
- negative_evidence: a conclusion that rests on something not being detected or on non-significance, without a shown sensitivity or equivalence margin.
- hypothesis_fit_and_scope: whether the test addresses the stated question, and whether the conclusion extends beyond the system, outcome or time window measured.

For each reported item add the consequence for the claim the experiment serves, and a proportionate remedy: clarify the reporting, reanalyse the existing data, narrow the claim, or add a discriminating experiment (only when the first three cannot resolve it). Report at most five items, the ones that matter most for how far this experiment supports the claim it serves. Skip minor reporting details (for example an exact time point or concentration) unless leaving them out limits the interpretation; do not list an item as ok unless that is informative. Use add_experiment only when clarifying, reanalysing or narrowing the claim cannot resolve the concern. Write finished sentences, no reasoning steps. Use no double quotation marks inside text values; quote wording with single quotation marks or paraphrase, and in the quote field leave double quotation marks out.
